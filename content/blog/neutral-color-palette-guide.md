Neutrals get a bad rap for being boring. Done right, they're the most versatile foundation in home decor.

## The core palette

| Role | Color | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| Base | Warm white / cream | Walls, large textiles |
| Mid-tone | Oatmeal / taupe | Sofas, rugs |
| Accent | Terracotta / rust | Pillows, ceramics |
| Depth | Charcoal / warm brown | Frames, lamp bases |
| Fresh | Sage / olive | Plants, small accents |

## Why warm beats cool

Cool grays can feel clinical. Warm neutrals — with yellow or pink undertones — reflect light softly and pair beautifully with wood tones and natural materials.

## The 60-30-10 rule

- **60%** base (walls, large furniture)
- **30%** mid-tone (rugs, curtains, bedding)
- **10%** accent (pillows, art, decor objects)

Stick to this ratio and your room will feel cohesive even as you swap seasonal accents.
