A cozy living room isn't about expensive furniture — it's about texture, warmth, and intentional details. Here's where to start.

## 1. Layer textiles first

Throw blankets and pillow covers are the fastest way to soften a space. Stick to a neutral palette (cream, oatmeal, sage) and mix textures: linen, chunky knit, velvet.

## 2. Add warm lighting

Overhead lighting alone feels harsh. A table lamp with a linen shade on a side table creates the ambient glow that makes a room feel lived-in.

## 3. Bring in natural elements

A faux olive tree, woven baskets, or a wooden tray on the coffee table adds organic warmth without clutter.

## 4. Curate your walls

One statement piece — a macramé hanging or round mirror — is enough. Leave breathing room around it.

## 5. Edit ruthlessly

Cozy doesn't mean crowded. Remove one item for every new piece you add. Less visual noise = more calm.
