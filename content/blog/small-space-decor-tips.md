Small spaces demand smart choices. These five tricks deliver maximum impact without sacrificing style.

## Use mirrors strategically

Place a round mirror opposite a window to bounce natural light deep into the room. Gold or thin black frames keep the look airy.

## Go vertical

Floating shelves draw the eye upward and free up floor space. Display books and a single plant per shelf — don't overcrowd.

## Choose multi-purpose pieces

Storage baskets that look good on display. Ottomans with hidden storage. Every item should earn its square footage.

## Keep the color story tight

Two to three colors max in a small room. Too many competing tones make walls feel like they're closing in.

## Let light through

Sheer curtains or no curtains at all. Heavy drapes absorb light and visual space.
